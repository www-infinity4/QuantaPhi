Frequently asked questions

Is QuantaPhi only an AI chatbot?

No. QuantaPhi brings a research interface, source links, media cards, story tools and experimental creation studios into one site. Some tools have separate pages; others appear within the main search experience.

Are the image and video generators always available?

Availability depends on the installed provider and its working API or rendering connection. If a provider is offline or unavailable, the interface may offer planning or preview features without completing a generation.

Where do I find the AI voice assistant?

Open the dedicated Bubbles AI page. Voice support depends on the browser or Android app permissions and the current service configuration.

Where can I learn more about Quants?

Read Search to Quants for the concept and visit the About page for an overview. Token balances and payouts must be checked against actual wallet receipts, not inferred from button clicks.
