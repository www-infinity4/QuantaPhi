QUANT AI · PRODUCTION CONCEPT

Automated Banking & Business Agent

A robot at the switchboard. Bubbles in motion. Quant AI’s film follows a humanoid business agent and its compact robot partner as they interpret indexed data, connect the right information and prepare useful business outputs.

Music direction: Shiny Happy People · R.E.M. Individual quants gather, connect and laugh; Share and Collect carry useful information into commerce. The fields of a page become a garden of extracted data and new inventory, with Infinity Platinum shining through the connected-hands sequence.

  1. Read the data: hundreds of indexed words appear across a Cloudflare-inspired information wall.
  2. Make the connections: the robots discuss meaning, then route information from A to B through a luminous switchboard.
  3. Send Bubbles into action: specialist bubbles research, read and assemble customer-relevant advertising drafts.
  4. Build the output: product cards, inventory proposals and order drafts appear for the business owner to review.

This is the film’s planned workflow. Banking, ad placement and orders require connected services and the owner’s authorization.
