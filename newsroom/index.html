How the News Phi desk works

QuantaPhi reports are new explanatory writing grounded in linked primary sources. When an agency announces a plan, we say it is a plan, rather than claiming the projected benefit has already occurred. Stories can stay in the archive as dated reports; they are not relabeled as fresh news.

Live headlines are a separate layer supplied by News Phi's existing Cloudflare feed. A source timestamp and HTTPS publisher link are required for display. If the service has no recent verified material or is unavailable, readers see a clear status rather than recycled headlines.

We don't claim commercial affiliation with the news organizations we cite. Editors should review new article drafts for accuracy, originality, source rights, sensitive subject matter and corrections before making them public. The current launch contains two original reports, not a promise that a fully automatic writing pipeline is running.
