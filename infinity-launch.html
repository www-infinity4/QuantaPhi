UPGRADE IN PROGRESS

Reliable connected sign-in

Make returning from official Google, GitHub, Cloudflare, and future provider login flows predictable, with wallet-scoped connection readback and clear consent.

PARTNER ACCESS REQUIRED

More StarMaker inside the card

We want API-authorized song results, licensed playback, personal selections, duets and social actions. Until StarMaker approves these, buttons hand off to the real app.

PAYMENT PARTNERS NOT CONFIRMED

Visa, Chime and checkout

Future commerce could use approved payment processors and integrations with transparent prices, per-user confirmation and receipts. Neither Visa nor Chime is represented as an active QuantaPhi payment partner.

RESEARCH DIRECTION

Software and physical robots

Advance testable software automation first. Investigate printable transformable delivery robots as a future engineering concept, not a launched delivery fleet.
