Discuss with BubblesScience Lab overview

Keep three physical ideas separate

  • Oxide host: the base material, for example aluminum oxide (Al₂O₃), boron oxide (B₂O₃), or titanium dioxide (TiO₂).
  • Dopant: an introduced element or impurity whose concentration and lattice site can change material properties. It must not be assumed active without measurement.
  • Oxygen vacancies: missing oxygen atoms in a solid's lattice. Their concentrations and charge states can affect electronic or ionic transport; a vacancy is not automatically a dopant.

Experimental proposal: compare oxide compositions, lattice defects, resistance switching and retention under controlled conditions. Do not assume Al₂O₃/B₂O₃ mixtures create a working computer without fabrication and validated electrical measurements.
