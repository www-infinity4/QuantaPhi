Connect this page to your TV

A browser cannot open Android's system screen-mirroring picker directly. Google Cast normally needs the phone and TV on the same Wi-Fi network.

  1. Connect the phone and a Cast-enabled TV/Chromecast to the same Wi-Fi. Mobile data alone normally cannot discover it.
  2. On Android, swipe down twice and tap Screen Cast / Cast / Smart View (or find screen casting in Google Home).
  3. Select the television and approve the connection. Then return to this page to watch the cards on the TV.
  4. For Netflix or paid channels, use the official provider's Cast button, if supported.
