Research boundaries

Public research only. Do not attempt restricted access, credentials, identity tracing, deanonymization, circumvention, site interference or automatic code changes.

Onion URLs are recorded as text, never navigated or tested by this tool. A public mirror, gateway or HypeStat estimate is not a measurement of the onion origin or its visitors. “Unavailable” means an unavailable observation was recorded at its timestamp, not that a site is down now.

Keep source statements distinct from your observations and analysis. Verification labels describe the cited evidence only; they are not operator or visitor verification. Do not collect private personal data, sensitive media or unlawful material.

Screenshot fingerprints prove only that a particular local file has specific bytes. They do not establish capture origin, accuracy or ownership. Keep originals and a separate secure backup as appropriate.

Local backup and recovery

Export versioned JSON to a file you control. Import merges unique IDs; conflicting IDs are rejected, never overwritten. This does not synchronize other devices.

Source context

HypeStat-style checks are manually recorded source estimates. Authorized site analytics (GA4, Search Console or Cloudflare) belong in the main HypeStat traffic workspace and must be kept separate from third-party reports.
