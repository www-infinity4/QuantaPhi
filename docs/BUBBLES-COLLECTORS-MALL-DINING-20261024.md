# Owner work order: premium collector, auction, library, media, mall and restaurant cards
Date: 2026-10-24. Source of truth: `data/bubbles-collector-mall-dining-cards-20261024.json` (19 individually specified card manifests).
Status: **requested/design ready, not an implemented or deployed set of cards**.

## Purpose and system-level outcome
Build a network of **genuine interactive branded cards** that preserves QuantaPhi's full-width Oracle "Choose a direction" rotation (Public / For You / Following), with the visual/function standard of the existing Google search and Walmart pizza cards. Make every card compelling enough to use on its own, not a shelf of logo tiles or external links.

A visitor researching a Morgan dollar should move seamlessly from **VAMWorld variety identification** to **PCGS / NGC coin research** to **actual Heritage past auctions**, collecting the precisely identified item and comparing *dated, real* evidence. Someone interested in vintage music culture can explore Morrison Hotel Gallery imagery, Rolling Stone coverage, Library of Congress archival sources, and collector histories. A shopper can swipe **premium Shop LC products** with actual SKU/product image/availability/price, save the selected product, then purchase only at its source. A diner can Star and Follow Sonic, Burger King or McDonald's to find **current verified** offers.

## Mandatory for EVERY completed card
- Verified and properly attributed real product photography, museum object, album/cover, coin image, brand logo or permitted media. Locate an appealing hero image. No fake logos, generated imitation of third-party branding, blank tiles or unrelated stock photos. A photo's availability on the internet is not a license to redistribute it. Use linked/approved source media, licensed embeds, or request reuse permission; protect rights metadata.
- Deliver **real in-card interaction** for this particular website/subject (a search box that searches the appropriate source, variation filter, item carousel, dated article/auction media player or coupon selection). Preserve official destinations and source identity; a plain website link is not enough.
- Shared controls: **Star / Collect / Share / + Follow / +1 Build / Quick Search**; item-level stable ID and current selection state; no duplicated toolbar. Star/Collect/follow states survive navigation and reload through the existing intended store. Shared URL opens the same public item with artwork and Open Graph tags; do not leak private account data.
- Share via native phone share, clipboard or Copy link. Embed the live card on cooperating third-party sites via documented frame/manifest interface with safe CSP. On X and other sites that don't allow arbitrary scripts, show faithful link/preview opening the actual working card. Never claim it is in-feed interactive when it is only an X link preview.
- Original Oracle Octaves white cards/warm background/red action accents, large readable typography, accessible controls on Moto G Pure, **single full-sized card**, swipe/next/previous functionality, actual image/video loaded and real useful interaction. Do not replace pages with tiny static card tiles.
- Quant learning must use voluntary Star/Follow/Collect/click categories and aggregated view+action counts; distinguish impressions and clicked offers from **people who ate there** or completed purchases. Following a restaurant is a declared interest, NOT proof of a visit, meal, identity or coupon redemption. Require affirmative opt-in and a clear disable option for alerts, and never sell personal dining inference without separately informed consent.
- Offers and auction/product data must be verified at source, with **source URL, fetched/checked timestamp, actual item ID, currency, active/expired status, date/time and eligibility**. No fabricated coupon codes, prices, certification/population figures, sales, private account access, or customer identity. Show an explicit unavailable state if data/usage permission is missing.
- Bubbles specialist genuinely researches each selected item, explains relevant evidence, follows citation/rights constraints, and tests it. Default work happens through existing authenticated Brain runner/PR/test/review, not unverified requests or a fake done receipt. Respect wallet/coin/payment rules and Android APK security hold #525.

## Research and build groups
1. **VAMWorld, NGC, PCGS and Heritage** (priority first): inspect current existing `vam-*`, `pcgs`, `ngc` catalog entries and `quanta-feed-card.html`. Upgrade them in place; do not duplicate. Need die markers, vintage Morgan close-ups, search year/mint/date, verification/census links, comparable sold auction lots and no false grading result.
2. **Shop LC Premium** (priority first): reuse existing Shop LC/Shop Phi work and check approved official premium listings. Rotate one authentic high-value item at a time; real product photography, SKU, details, transparent price/status. Do not confuse asking price and independent value. Use product-level Collect/Share/Star.
3. **Topps, Beckett, Christie's, Library of Congress**: player/set checklists and official grader information, authenticated auction lot search/results, LOC collections with citations/rights. No provider paywall bypass or unlicensed archive mirrors.
4. **Morrison Hotel Gallery, Rolling Stone, COINage and Coin World**: original publisher/photographer identity, exceptional music/coin article imagery, real story lists and issue/collection search, credited source. Resolve ambiguous separate "Coins magazine" rather than treating every possible magazine as the same publisher.
5. **Sonic, Burger King, McDonald's, then other popular restaurants**: brand-shaped food advertisement cards with actual offer selection, a coupon/rewards **source open**, Star/+ Follow future offers, clear end date and app-only/participating-store conditions. Only surface live verified offers; subscriptions and local store preferences explicitly opt-in. No inferred dining history, location tracking, forged coupons or fake redemption.
6. **Adjacent sources**: CAC, Stack's Bowers, ANA, Whitman, Newman Numismatic Portal only after official current URLs, artwork permissions, and concrete use cases are confirmed.
7. Reconcile with earlier **Guess, Levi's, Pepe Jeans, Maurices, Sam Goody, record-store, Walmart, Spencer's, Radio Shack** mall backlog rather than silently dropping them. Each still requires official photos/logos and a functioning product/category carousel before admission.
