## External Gmail is not a Gmail OAuth grant for Bubbles
- The owner has external Gmail accounts connected to ChatGPT. **Neither connection is automatically an authorization for Bubbles or Quant Mail.** No recovery factor is approved until that specific address receives and successfully verifies a fresh domain-independent challenge **within QuantaPhi**, or completes approved Google OAuth plus verified-email binding. Do not publish users' private recovery addresses, including the owner's, in issues, source code, logs, or public change requests.
- Never copy ChatGPT's Gmail connector tokens, refresh tokens, password, or cookies into Workers or wallets. Neither Gmail's profile name nor a user-supplied email string verifies account control.
- The owner should choose which independent Gmail address(es) to enroll when the recovery enrollment UI and sender are actually operational. The selected external mailbox must keep functioning even if QuantaPhi's domain, website or Cloudflare mail route is down. It cannot grant access while QuantaPhi's recovery verifier is unavailable; account-state and challenge results persist safely for eventual recheck.
- Administrator and owner mailbox claims require already verified owner identity and admin-role checks, not first-come public signup. Never expose internal owner IDs, addresses, receipts, or recovery factor lists to unverified users.

## Six user-enrolled verification methods (three verified proofs for high-risk recovery)
1. Primary WebAuthn passkey, registered on `quantaphi.org` with validated RP ID, origin, fresh challenge, user verification and counter/credential checks.
2. Backup **independent** security key or device passkey, separate authenticator provenance. A second passkey synced from the same account/device is not independent for counting two factors.
3. Verified **external** recovery email (never solely `@quantaphi.org`) and single-use email challenge.
4. Verified TOTP authenticator app, cryptographic secret stored encrypted and never sent to browser after enrollment.
5. Verified telephone number with approved SMS/voice challenge. SIM-swap/spoofing risk is high; never sufficient alone.
6. Offline one-use recovery code generated with a CSPRNG, displayed once, hashed at rest and atomically consumed.

Enforce enrollment only after existing authenticated wallet/passkey, confirmation of the new factor, risk review and owner notifications for newly added/removed factors. Warn before activating 3-of-6 policy when fewer than three independent methods are enrolled. Enforce **three independently verified methods** for recovery, including at least one passkey/security key for automatic **eligibility**. The three proof receipts must be bound to one user, one server-generated attempt, independent authenticators and unique challenges, be unexpired and not reused. If all passkeys are gone, three weaker factors are **not** automatic access: manual delayed high-assurance recovery with notices to previously registered contacts, safe freeze of high-risk actions and audited review. Never imply that policy evaluation issues sessions.

The module `workers/quant-mail/recovery-communication-policy.mjs` is a **pure internal eligibility guard**, not an API route or cryptographic WebAuthn/OTP verifier. It expects trust established by the server. Before anyone can use it to recover an account, implement audited verifier adapters, immutable enrolled factor identifiers and proof state, atomic one-time challenges, transactionally consumed offline codes, idempotent recovery sessions, throttles, anti-enumeration, time-limited responses, alerts, and full per-wallet isolation. Protect wallet credentials, Quants, Infinity tokens, Star Coins, chats and old receipts; never reset balances to implement recovery. Privileged owner recovery requires independent review and live phone acceptance before deploy. Avoid enabling any public route until such verification exists.

## Proposed Bubbles account recovery UI
Accounts → Security & Recovery in a **separate viewer** from the chat composer. Show six method cards, `not enrolled` / `verified` / `revoked` states **from trusted server receipts**, not browser toggles. Distinguish `Kris/Administrator mailbox not live` and `External Gmail pending verification`. Permit user to enroll a new factor, review last use/revocation, download fresh offline codes once, and request recovery. Never auto-sign-in or show “Recovered” on three clicked boxes; passkey challenge has to complete on the user's device. Bubbles/Robot Brain cannot bypass or export a passkey. For agent actions on other apps, use user-consented OAuth with scoped tokens or platform-approved service access, not the user's WebAuthn credentials.

## Quant AI communications switchboard (separate privilege from account recovery)
Components: verified per-user Quant Mail Inbox → opt-in AI triage/draft/summarization → sender identity + recipients/consent verifier → deduplication/outbox → authorized provider adapters (email, phone, SMS, X replies) → delivery/call receipts → Bubbles notification. Store each user's messages in their own partition, not model training by default. Allow manual human takeover, refusal/stop, quiet hours, scheduled cancellation, call logs when lawful and minimal data retention. One user never uses the owner's Gmail, X, or wallet grants.

- AI may handle an inbound customer inquiry or conversation only under the user's scoped permission and accurate AI identity disclosure. User-initiated customer conversations are the low-risk initial launch.
- Outbound advertising, sales calls and SMS to people without prior permission are not enabled. Collect seller-specific, purpose-specific consent, DNC suppression, and (when required) prior written agreement. In the US, AI-generated calls are regulated as artificial/prerecorded voice under the TCPA, and telemarketing has additional restrictions. Comply with all relevant federal/state calling, recording and opt-out laws, not a blanket business-owner enable switch.
- AI may search original manufacturer specs, verify availability/price, match a customer's accessibility/installation needs, and invite an **opted-in** customer to talk with a human. The walk-in bathing example is a scenario, **not** a verified government-funded procurement program, active manufacturer agreement, 1,000× sales result or robot factory order. Do not represent it as such. After verified leads, aggregate demand signals for manufacturers with legitimate permission. Robotics capacity decisions remain real companies' decisions.
- AI does not commit payments, sign contracts, order manufacturing, claim affiliate relationships, change government benefits or authorize bulk marketing without verifiable specific permission and normal review.
