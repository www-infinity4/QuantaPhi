Phone numbers for websites

A phone number can be tied to a verified business Quick Card, website, and Bubbles-powered receptionist—using a real, licensed telecommunications provider.

01 · Use existing verified numbers

Link the business's published phone number or opt-in contact channel without creating a new number or impersonating its owner.

02 · Provision a new business line

After owner verification and telecom onboarding, search available numbers, obtain user approval for provider fees, then provision the number and configure inbound voice routing.

03 · AI receptionist + messages

Incoming call → verified carrier webhook → tenant-specific switchboard → business-approved greeting → optional human transfer. SMS requires registration and user consent.

04 · Protect customers

Never invent a working number; never silently capture calls, spoof caller IDs, accept payment card details through an unapproved handler, or silently enable recordings. Include opt-outs, deletion and emergency-call limitations.

Provider activation: not connected

Twilio documents the search → provision → webhook path. New numbers cost money and require your provider account, consent and regulatory checks.
