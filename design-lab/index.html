      WHY THIS CARD EXISTS

      A circuit with a purpose.

      Every new Quick Card begins with intent, an actual working mechanism, and proof.

      01 / REASON

      Understand intent

      A visible, overridable intent router recommends a source circuit. It doesn't secretly decide what to send or buy.

      02 / ENGINE

      Operate, don't imitate

      One button launches bounded public-index queries, not 40 browser popups. Stop cancels outstanding requests.

      03 / EVIDENCE

      Make every claim testable

      Original URLs, match counts and per-domain receipts distinguish a real result from empty data or a failed search.

      DESIGN TARGET / PHASE 21,000-node orchestration grid

      Server-side queued jobs, domain rate limits, robots and publisher terms, cancellation, provenance and reusable worker receipts. Not deployed in prototype 001.
