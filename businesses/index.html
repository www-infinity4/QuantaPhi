How records are verified

Listings, cards and advertising are different

Public business facts can be listed without the owner buying advertising. Infinity cards may link to an original store or display appropriately licensed source images. Sponsored placements, claimed ownership, real inventory, and direct checkout require separate verification and authorization. A city in the expansion queue is not counted as populated.

Source attribution appears in every record. The Cloudflare directory maintains import receipts and retains source identifiers to prevent duplicate cards. You can always follow the original source before shopping or visiting.
