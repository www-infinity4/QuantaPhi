Questions to consider before investing

  • Cashflow and targets: What assumptions support the projected cashflow, and how are distributions funded?
  • Liquidity: When and under what conditions could an investor withdraw or sell an interest?
  • Fees and conflicts: What management, acquisition, financing and disposition fees appear in the actual offering documents?
  • Eligibility: Does the specific fund require accredited investor verification under Regulation D, or follow a different exemption?
  • Risk: How do leverage, occupancy, maintenance, insurance and market changes affect returns?

These are research questions, not claims about any fund's current terms. Refer to the offering documents, not an AI-generated estimate, for the legal details.
