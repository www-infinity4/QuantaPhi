## Overview

QuantaPhi combines rigorous scientific experimentation with modular robot design. All results, schematics, and step‑by‑step guides are published openly so anyone can reproduce the work.

## Scientific Methodology

1. **Hypothesis Definition** – Clearly state the research question.
2. **Experimental Design** – Choose variables, controls, and measurement tools.
3. **Data Collection** – Use calibrated sensors on our robot platforms.
4. **Analysis** – Generate graphs and statistical summaries.
5. **Conclusion & Iteration** – Refine the hypothesis and repeat.

The full description of each step lives on the website under the **Science** section.

## Robot Engineering Workflow

1. **Concept Sketch** – Ideate the robot’s purpose and form factor.
2. **CAD Modeling** – Build a 3‑D model using open‑source tools.
3. **Fabrication** – 3‑D print or CNC‑machine parts.
4. **Electronics Integration** – Wire sensors, actuators, and the Boron safe‑wallet controller.
5. **Software Stack** – Deploy firmware, telemetry, and autonomous routines.
6. **Testing & Validation** – Run the experiment suite and record data.

Detailed tutorials are available in the `science/tutorials/` directory.

## Data Visualisation & Graphs

All charts are rendered as SVGs to keep the site lightweight and fully offline‑compatible. The design follows the **Oracle Octaves** visual language:

- **Paper**: `#f7f4f0`
- **Ink**: `#312722`
- **Accent Red**: `#b83b32`
- Rounded corners, 44 px touch targets, and accessible focus outlines.
