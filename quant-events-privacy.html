Interaction analytics preferences

Choose whether QuantaPhi may save your signed-in Quick Card views and actions to improve its own card experience. This is optional. The setting applies only to the proposed first-party Quant event collection service.

It does not control existing Google Analytics, AdSense, or your Wallet transaction records. Enabling this setting does not mint, spend or transfer coins. Location collection stays off.
